Sipling — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 July 2026 · App version 1.0

Sipling collects, stores and shares no personal data.

Everything you enter — your height, weight, age, what you drink, your trees — stays in your phone's own storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, because the app has no permission to access the internet.

Sipling has no server. You are never asked to create an account. Your advertising ID is not read. No analytics or crash reporting tools are used.

What data do we process?

DataWhere it livesWhy
Profile: sex, age, height, weight, activity level, waking hours On your phone only To calculate your daily water goal
Your drinks: amount, drink type, time On your phone only To show your progress, your streak and your forest
Preferences: reminder times, cups, theme On your phone only To run the app the way you want

You can erase all of it at any time with Settings → Reset all data. Uninstalling the app does the same. There is no copy on our side, so you never need to send us a deletion request.

Health Connect

An optional feature you can enable in Settings writes the water you drink to Android's shared health record (Health Connect). This feature is off by default and nothing is written until you turn it on and grant permission.

  • Sipling only writes. It never reads anything from Health Connect — the read permission is not even declared.
  • The only thing written is the amount and time of the liquid you drank (Hydration).
  • This data never leaves your phone; Health Connect also runs on your device.
  • You can turn the feature off in Settings and revoke the permission in the Health Connect app. Records already written can be deleted from Health Connect.

Permissions and why they are requested

PermissionWhy
POST_NOTIFICATIONSTo show water reminders
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDTo re-create reminders after the phone restarts
VIBRATEReminder vibration (can be turned off in Settings)
health.WRITE_HYDRATIONThe optional Health Connect feature described above
WAKE_LOCK, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Required by Android's work manager so the home screen widget can run in the background. They do not grant internet access.

The app has no INTERNET permission. Location, camera, microphone, contacts and file permissions are not requested either.

Children

Sipling is suitable for all ages and, because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.
